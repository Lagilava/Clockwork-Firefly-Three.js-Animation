Sculptor the director's desk

The film is a pure function of time, so everything here is live: scrub the timeline and the viewport, the director's-cut monitor and the curves all follow. Your work autosaves in this browser.

  1. Pick a shot in the list on the left, or click the timeline. The monitor shows what the camera sees.
  2. Edit its camera with E: drag the gizmos in the viewport, or on the map (M) for the big picture.
  3. Grade the picture in the Grade tab. Use the shot scope to colour one shot, or load a .cube LUT.
  4. Try ideas safely. Save a version in the Versions tab, then flip between A and B with \.
  5. Share it. Export a clip or stills of any range, or press Play in film to watch the whole cut.
